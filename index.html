Privacy Policy for Dad Workout Timer

Last updated: July 10, 2025

Information We Collect

Dad Workout Timer stores your workout data locally on your device, including:

  • Workout history and progress
  • Exercise preferences and settings
  • XP points and level progression

How We Use Your Information

All data is stored locally on your device to:

  • Track your fitness progress
  • Save your workout preferences
  • Provide a personalized experience

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely on your device and is not transmitted to external servers. We do not collect, store, or share your personal information.

Third-Party Services

This app does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or data collection services.

Children's Privacy

Our app is suitable for all ages and does not collect personal information from children.
