Frequently Asked Questions

How do I track my progress?

Your workout history and XP progress are automatically saved and can be viewed in the Dashboard.

Can I add custom exercises?

How do streaks work?

Complete your weekly workout target (3 workouts for beginners, 6 for advanced) to maintain your streak!
